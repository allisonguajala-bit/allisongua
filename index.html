About me

Allison Guajala Maldonado

Engineer, researcher, and organizer. I work where climate risk, water, and energy meet the communities who carry the most of it.

I started my career as a mechanical engineer at Pratt & Whitney, designing and testing commercial jet engine components. I came to UC Berkeley's Development Engineering program to put those skills toward climate resilience and environmental justice, with a focus on Latin America and underrepresented urban communities like the ones I come from in Union City, New Jersey.

Today I combine GIS, data analysis, and participatory research, and I work in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.
